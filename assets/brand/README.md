# Viewoupe brand pack

Final identity for the Viewoupe 0.1 public baseline.

## Core idea

The wordmark stays readable first. The thin blue vertical stroke hints at the missing **L** in *View + Loupe*. The short diagonal stroke extends from the final **e**, making the letter itself behave like a magnifier rather than adding a separate search icon.

## Primary assets

- `viewoupe-logo-light.svg` — primary transparent wordmark for light surfaces.
- `viewoupe-logo-dark.svg` — dark-surface lockup.
- `viewoupe-icon.svg` — compact app/repository icon.
- `favicon.svg` — simplified small-size mark.
- `viewoupe-social-card.svg` — 1280×640 social / repository preview.
- PNG exports mirror the SVG masters where present.

## Rules

- Official name in prose and metadata: **Viewoupe**.
- Stylized wordmark: `view|oupe`.
- Keep the separator thin; it must not split the word visually.
- Keep the final `e` at normal size in the primary mark.
- The handle must remain short and light; the `e` is the lens.
- Do not add a full magnifier circle, box, glow, or heavy frame to the wordmark.
- Functional in-page activator remains `VL`; it is not the main brand icon.

## Palette

Charcoal `#0F172A` · Slate `#64748B` · Accent blue `#3B82F6` · Background `#F8FAFC`.
